OUR SYSTEMATIC WORKFLOW

Four-Stage Timber Engineering Protocol

We combine mechanical engineering principles with carpenter craftsmanship to ensure 100% transit security:

01

Laser Dimensioning & Center of Gravity Audit

Our engineers calculate the exact center of gravity, slinging points, and vulnerable contact surfaces to prevent rotational tipping during lifting and transit.

02

Kiln-Drying & Certified ISPM-15 Treatment

Timber is chamber-heated to 56°C for a minimum of 30 continuous minutes to eliminate wood pests, stamped with traceable IPPC marks for international compliance.

03

Shock-Damping & VCI Barrier Assembly

Cargo is wrapped with heavy-duty VCI foil, surrounded by high-density polyethylene foam saddles, and anchored to internal wooden blocks with steel hardware.

04

Heavy-Duty Strapping & Container Lashing

The completed wooden case is reinforced with high-tensile steel straps, impact indicators, and secured inside ocean containers using certified polyester cord lashing.

GOT QUESTIONS?

Frequently Asked Questions About Wooden Packaging

Answers to common questions regarding custom crating, export certifications, and on-site crating services:

What is ISPM-15 certification and why is it mandatory for export wooden crates?
ISPM-15 (International Standards for Phytosanitary Measures No. 15) is an internationally recognized standard developed by the IPPC to prevent the global spread of timber pests. Every wooden crate leaving India must undergo certified heat treatment (HT) at 56°C for 30 minutes and be stamped with a registered IPPC mark. Failure to comply can result in immediate cargo rejection, quarantine fines, or return of the container at customs.
Can DHLR Express construct custom wooden crates on-site at our factory or premises?
Yes. We deploy fully equipped mobile crating teams directly to your manufacturing facility, warehouse, data center, or residence across Pan-India. Our crew carries pre-treated certified timber, portable power saws, pneumatic nailers, and protective liners to build bespoke crates around your equipment on-site.
How are sensitive electronics and delicate marble pieces protected inside wooden boxes?
We isolate sensitive cargo using customized multi-density polyethylene (EPE/EVA) foam buffers that dampen vibration. For moisture-sensitive electronics and precision tools, we apply hermetically heat-sealed aluminum barrier foil bags with active desiccant packs to maintain 0% relative humidity. Marble and glass items are suspended in floating wooden frames lined with non-abrasive felt.
What is the difference between open-slat crates and fully enclosed plywood boxes?
Open-slat (skeleton) crates feature spaced timber slats that provide structural rigidity and stacking strength while keeping freight weight low. They are ideal for durable machinery parts and domestic shipping. Fully enclosed plywood boxes have solid walls on all 6 sides, providing complete defense against dust, moisture, and theft—essential for overseas ocean freight and high-value cargo.
