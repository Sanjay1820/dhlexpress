Containerized transport represents the gold standard for secure, weatherproof freight and household relocation. DHLR Express maintains a specialized fleet of closed-body container trucks and ISO modular containers, replacing obsolete open-body tarpaulin trucks to eliminate rain damage, highway dust, and pilferage risks.

We provide dedicated 20-foot, 24-foot, and 32-foot high-cube containers for complete villa moves and full-truckload (FTL) commercial shipments, as well as partitioned shared containers for economical consolidated moving. OUR SYSTEMATIC APPROACH

Four-Stage Containerized Transport Protocol

Ensuring end-to-end security and punctuality from origin pickup to destination unloading:

01

Pre-Loading Moisture & Structural Inspection

Every container floor and wall undergoes clean-dry-sound (CDS) inspection to verify complete water-tightness and clean interior surfaces prior to loading.

02

Systematic Tiered Loading & Internal Lashing

Heavy goods and furniture are anchored at floor level using nylon ratchet straps, followed by lighter cartons stacked with load-distributing dividers.

03

Origin Bolt Sealing with Verified Serial Codes

Container rear doors are locked with heavy tamper-evident ISO 17712 bolt seals. The unique serial code is recorded on your consignment consignment note.

04

GPS Telemetry & Destination Seal Verification

Transit progress is tracked via real-time satellite telemetry. Upon doorstep arrival, the customer verifies the intact seal code before cutting the bolt.

GOT QUESTIONS?

Frequently Asked Questions About Container Transport

Answers to common questions regarding closed container moving, security seals, and part-load options:

Why is a closed container truck safer than a conventional open-body truck?
Open-body trucks rely on tarpaulins that can leak during heavy downpours, flap in highway winds causing friction, and leave goods vulnerable to dust and roadside tampering. Closed steel containers are 100% waterproof, locked with metal bolt seals, and provide a rigid steel fortress protecting your assets across interstate highways.
How does the tamper-evident bolt seal work?
Before the vehicle departs your location, our supervisor seals the rear locking hasp with a heavy steel bolt stamped with a unique alphanumeric serial number (e.g. DHLR-89214). This number is signed on your consignment note. The seal can only be severed with a heavy-duty bolt cutter in your presence at the destination, ensuring zero access during transit.
What is the difference between Dedicated and Shared Container service?
Dedicated container service assigns the entire vehicle exclusively to your shipment, allowing direct non-stop transport on your preferred schedule. Shared container service consolidates your goods with other clients headed to the same city; each client's items are separated and tagged, making it significantly more economical for smaller loads.
Can I track my container's location during highway transit?
Yes. All DHLR Express container vehicles are equipped with active GPS transponders. You receive periodic milestone SMS/WhatsApp updates and can contact your dedicated move coordinator 24/7 for exact real-time vehicle positioning.
