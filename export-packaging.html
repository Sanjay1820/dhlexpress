GOT QUESTIONS?

Frequently Asked Questions About Export Packaging

Authoritative guidance on international freight packing, customs inspections, and sea voyage defense:

How does vacuum foil packaging protect machinery during long oceanic transit?
Ocean containers endure severe humidity shifts and "container rain" caused by day/night temperature drops over open seas. Multi-layer aluminum barrier foil is completely impermeable to water vapor. By vacuum-sealing the machinery with calibrated silica gel desiccant bags, internal relative humidity remains below 30%, which mathematically makes rust and oxidation impossible for up to two full years.
What documentation and certification is provided with DHLR Express export packaging?
We provide complete Phytosanitary Fumigation and ISPM-15 Heat-Treatment certificates with official IPPC numbers, Certificate of Packing and Seaworthy Lashing for marine cargo insurance underwriters, and hazardous material container compliance certificates where applicable.
Can DHLR Express execute container stuffing and lashing at our factory warehouse?
Yes. We send specialized mobile stuffing crews equipped with pneumatic nailers, heavy hardwood timber, cord strapping tensioners, and high-flow dunnage airbag inflators directly to your premises. We coordinate seamlessly with your container trailers and container dispatch schedules.
What are dunnage airbags and why are they necessary inside ocean containers?
Dunnage airbags are heavy-duty poly-woven inflatable bladders placed into empty void spaces between heavy wooden crates or pallets. Once pressurized with air, they lock the cargo against the container walls, absorbing oceanic pitch and roll forces and preventing destructive impact damage during maritime transport.
