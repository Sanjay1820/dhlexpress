Door-to-Door Household Relocation Services

Moving your home is more than changing an address; it represents a personal milestone that deserves utmost care and discipline. DHLR Express provides turnkey household relocation solutions across town and between major Indian cities, managing every detail from preliminary site surveys and custom packing to secure containerized transit and final white-glove placement.

Our certified moving specialists pack delicate chinaware, kitchen utensils, electronics, and wardrobe apparel using virgin-grade 5-ply corrugated cartons, heavy bubble cushioning, and waterproof stretch wraps. Bulky modular furniture, beds, and dining tables are disassembled by trained carpenters, cushioned with edge guards, and reassembled flawlessly in your new rooms.

Your possessions travel inside sealed, GPS-monitored container trucks backed by comprehensive transit insurance coverage and dedicated 24/7 moving desk assistance, ensuring you settle into your new home with complete peace of mind.

Organized Office & Corporate Relocation

Minimizing business downtime is critical during commercial transitions. DHLR Express executes seamless office relocations and large-scale corporate campus transfers with military-grade precision, often scheduled overnight or over weekends so your teams resume operations on Monday morning without interruption.

We deploy specialized anti-static packaging for data servers, networking racks, executive workstations, conference displays, and sensitive digital archives. Each department's inventory receives color-coded asset labeling matching the architectural destination floor plan, eliminating post-move chaos.

From cubicle dismantling and ergonomic seating transfers to secure record archival and IT decommissioning, our corporate project managers supervise every phase with milestone accountability.

Safe & Specialized Car Carrier Transportation

DHLR Express provides premier, damage-free car transportation and two-wheeler carrier services across India. Utilizing dedicated enclosed auto trailers equipped with heavy-duty wheel stoppers, hydraulic ramps, and safety harnesses, we ensure your vehicle is insulated from road gravel, dust, and climatic variations throughout its journey.

Whether transporting a family hatchback, luxury sedan, SUV, or premium motorcycle, your vehicle receives individualized attention. We offer single-car covered trailers as well as shared multi-tier carriers for cost-optimized regional and intercity transit.

Every vehicle undergoes a detailed pre-loading inspection with photographic documentation and condition reports. Our GPS-monitored carriers follow standardized safety stops every 700 to 800 km, ensuring strict compliance with scheduled delivery timelines and driver rest cycles.

With door-to-door pickup, real-time transit status updates, and comprehensive insurance coverage options, DHLR Express delivers total peace of mind for your prized vehicles.

Certified Packing Solutions & Custom Crating

Superior packaging is the core foundation of a zero-damage move. DHLR Express utilizes certified, industrial-grade packing materials specifically engineered to withstand transit vibrations, rough handling, and environmental exposure across long-distance highways and air/sea corridors.

Our material inventory includes 5-ply and 7-ply virgin corrugated boxes, dense polyethylene bubble rolls, acid-free tissue paper for silverware, telescopic wardrobe cartons, and heavy moisture barriers. For high-value sculptures, marble fixtures, chandeliers, and machinery, our carpenters build ISPM-15 certified heat-treated wooden crates with internal shock-damping foam.

Pan-India Commercial Freight & Logistics

DHLR Express bridges enterprise shipping demands with a verified nationwide fleet of heavy-duty vehicles, containerized multi-axle trucks, and regional feeder networks. We provide scalable Full Truckload (FTL) and Part Truckload (PTL) logistics solutions engineered for dependability and cost efficiency.

Our logistics portfolio includes scheduled intercity cargo transport, heavy machinery transit, palletized goods distribution, and dedicated express linehauls. Every route is managed with computerized dispatch and continuous transit tracking to maintain strict delivery punctuality.

Enterprise Supply Chain & 3PL Distribution

From central distribution centers to last-mile customer fulfillment and reverse logistics, DHLR Express delivers integrated enterprise supply chain solutions and 3PL logistics management. We partner with manufacturers, distributors, and commercial enterprises to streamline inventory velocity and eliminate supply chain bottlenecks.

Our logistical consultants analyze your shipping patterns to design optimized distribution corridors, regional fulfillment hubs, and dedicated freight schedules that significantly lower operational overhead while improving delivery performance.

Contact our enterprise supply chain specialists today to receive a tailored logistics blueprint and commercial capability brochure.

Secure Warehousing & Flexible Storage

Our modern, climate-monitored warehousing and storage facilities cater to both household goods and commercial enterprise inventories. Featuring 24/7 CCTV surveillance, automated pest control, fire suppression systems, and dedicated private storage bays, we safeguard your assets for short-term or extended durations.

From family furniture and sensitive household electronics during transition periods to commercial raw materials, spare parts, and finished goods, our facilities provide clean, dry, and highly organized space.

Equipped with standardized ISO pallet racking, heavy-duty forklifts, and raised loading docks, our warehousing and storage facilities enable rapid loading and unloading with zero risk of handling damage.

Clients benefit from itemized barcode inventory tracking, flexible monthly tenure terms, and dedicated private lockable units tailored to individual capacity requirements.
