Our certified packing specialists utilize tailored material combinations to ensure optimal protection for diverse household and commercial assets:

✓High-density polyethylene bubble cushioning & protective foam rolls
✓Virgin-grade 5-ply & 7-ply heavy-duty cartons and sheets
✓Moisture-resistant cling wrap, stretch film, and heavy tarpaulins
✓Acid-free tissue wrapping paper for chinaware & fragile glassware
✓Volatile Corrosion Inhibitor (VCI) films & silica desiccant packs
✓ISPM-15 heat-treated export timber crates & plywood boxes

OUR SYSTEMATIC APPROACH

Four-Stage Scientific Packing Protocol

Every relocation handled by DHLR Express adheres to stringent protective packaging standards engineered to withstand long-haul road dynamics and humidity fluctuations:

01

Pre-Move Fragility Audit

Our certified packing surveyors catalog every possession, segmenting items by physical vulnerability, surface finish, and dynamic transit load requirements.

02

Multi-Layered Cushioning

Items receive dense bubble wrap, foam rolls, edge guards, and stretch wrap to shield fragile surfaces and absorb road shocks.

03

Custom Timber Crating

Fine art, flat screens, marble tabletops, and heavy machinery are encapsulated in ISPM-15 certified, heat-treated wooden crates built to exact dimensions.

04

Itemized Room Staging

Every carton receives high-contrast color-coded labels matching floor plans for rapid, effortless unpacking and furniture placement at your new destination.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Packing & Materials Questions Answered

Are packing materials included in the DHLR Express moving quote?
Yes. Our standard door-to-door relocation quotations include all certified virgin-grade cartons, bubble cushioning, sealing tapes, shrink wrap, and specialized tools required for a safe, damage-free move, with zero hidden material charges.
What is the difference between single-wall and double-wall cartons?
Single-wall cartons feature 3 paper layers and are suitable for lightweight goods. DHLR Express exclusively utilizes 5-ply and 7-ply double-wall corrugated cartons that deliver over 2.5× higher puncture resistance and vertical stack strength, ensuring boxes never collapse during transit.
How do you safeguard flat-screen televisions and fragile artworks?
Televisions and mirrors are first encased in non-abrasive foam sheets, cushioned in heavy bubble barrier wrap, and placed inside custom telescopic corrugated cartons or on-site built wooden crates with interior shock absorbers.
Do you assist with unpacking and debris clearance at destination?
Absolutely. Our white-glove moving crew unpacks your cartons, places furniture into your designated rooms, reassembles dismantled beds and tables, and removes all used cartons, bubble wrap, and packaging debris from your premises.
